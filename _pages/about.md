I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).
